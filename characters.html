The People Behind the Story

Meet the Characters

Characters bring a novel to life. Discover the people connected to the mystery at the heart of The House That Horror Built.

Main Characters

👩
Main Character

Harry Adams

Harry is a single mother who takes a cleaning job at the mansion of horror-film director Javier Castillo. She is trying to provide for her young daughter while dealing with an increasingly unusual situation.

Importance: Harry's experiences guide readers through the central mystery of the novel.

🎬
Important Character

Javier Castillo

Javier Castillo is a horror-film director and the owner of the mansion where Harry works. His connection to the world of horror makes him an important figure in the story.

Importance: His mansion and circumstances are central to Harry's new job and the novel's mystery.

🧒
Family Connection

Harry's Daughter

Harry's young daughter is an important part of Harry's life and her motivation to work. Her presence highlights Harry's responsibilities as a mother.

Importance: She helps readers understand what Harry is working to protect and provide for.

Character analysis: Harry's role as a mother gives her decisions a personal significance. Javier's position as the mansion's owner connects Harry's ordinary working life to the novel's unusual and unsettling setting.
