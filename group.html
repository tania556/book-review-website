Who We Are

Welcome to The Midnight Pages, a team of five enthusiastic readers who believe that every book has a story worth exploring.

Our group consists of Fatima, Tania, Ume Zahra, Kalsoom, and Humaira. Together, we have chosen The House That Horror Built by Christina Henry for our book-review project.

Through this website, we introduce readers to the novel, explore its setting and characters, summarize its story, and discuss its major themes. Our goal is to share our understanding of the book, encourage thoughtful reading, and help visitors discover what makes a story memorable.
